About MADOCS

A professional association that brings together doctors involved in circumcision practice in Malaysia.

Background

The Malaysian Doctor of Circumcision Society (MADOCS) is a professional body registered with the Registrar of Societies Malaysia. MADOCS brings together doctors involved in circumcision practice with the aim of raising clinical standards, patient safety and the quality of training.

Official content on the vision, mission and history of the organisation will be published here once approved by the committee.

Vision

To be the leading professional digital platform for doctors involved in circumcision practice in Malaysia.

Mission

  • To raise the standards and safety of circumcision practice through education and training.
  • To provide structured clinical training and continuous professional development.
  • To support knowledge sharing, research and professional networking among doctors.
  • To represent members' interests and support community health programmes.
