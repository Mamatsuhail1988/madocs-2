President’s Foreword

A message from the First President of MADOCS.

Dr. Abdul Rashid

First President

President · Term 2026–2028

Malaysian Doctor of Circumcision Society

  • Structured education
  • Clinical training
  • Knowledge sharing

Assalamualaikum warahmatullahi wabarakatuh and warm greetings.

Alhamdulillah, our highest gratitude to Allah SWT, whose grace continues to give us the space to serve the community through healthcare.

The Malaysian Doctor of Circumcision Society (MADOCS) was founded on the conviction that safe circumcision practice requires continuous knowledge, training and guidance. The society stands on three pillars: structured education, quality clinical training, and knowledge sharing among medical practitioners across the country.

This website was built as an official channel for members, medical practitioners and the public to obtain information on the society’s training programmes, activities and services. We intend it to be an accessible, accurate and regularly updated reference.

To the members and colleagues who have contributed their energy, time and expertise — thank you. To doctors who have not yet joined us, the doors of MADOCS are always open. Let us work together to raise the quality of patient care and the safety of circumcision practice in Malaysia.

May this effort bring lasting benefit to patients, their families and the entire medical profession in Malaysia.

Thank you.

Dr. Abdul Rashid
First President, Malaysian Doctor of Circumcision Society

About MADOCS

Established in 2022 — now four years old — the Malaysian Doctor of Circumcision Society (MADOCS) is a professional association bringing together doctors involved in circumcision practice, education and training in Malaysia.

About MADOCS

The Malaysian Doctor of Circumcision Society (MADOCS) is a professional association that raises the standards of safe circumcision practice through education, training, advocacy and professional collaboration.
