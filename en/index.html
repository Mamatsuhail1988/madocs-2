About MADOCS

The Malaysian Doctor of Circumcision Society (MADOCS) is a professional association that brings together doctors involved in the practice, education and training of circumcision in Malaysia.

We provide a structured clinical training platform, continuous professional development and knowledge sharing to raise the quality and safety of circumcision services for the community.

Get to know MADOCS

What We Do

Education & Training

A structured clinical training programme to enhance practitioners' skills and competency.

Clinical Standards

To support safe circumcision practice through guidelines and best standards.

Professional Development

To provide a CPD platform, professional support and continuous knowledge sharing.

Advocacy & Community

To represent members' interests and support CSR and community health programmes.
