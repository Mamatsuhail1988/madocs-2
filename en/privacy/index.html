Privacy Policy

The Malaysian Doctor of Circumcision Society (MADOCS) collects personal information submitted voluntarily through this site, for example through membership application forms, course registration or enquiry forms.

Information collected

  • Contact information (name, e-mail, telephone number).
  • Professional information (MMC registration number, place of practice, state).
  • Information you enter in a message or application.

Use of information

Information is used solely to process membership, training programme registration, enquiries and communication related to MADOCS activities.

Sharing

Personal information is not sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes. Member names and personal information are not published on the public site.

Retention

Information is retained only as long as necessary for the purposes above and protected in accordance with reasonable administrative practices.

Contact us

For requests to access, correct or delete data, contact info@madocs.my.

This document is a draft for committee review and will be updated in accordance with the applicable legal requirements, including the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (2024 amendment).

ABOUT MADOCS

The Malaysian Doctor of Circumcision Society (MADOCS) is a professional association that raises the standards of safe circumcision practice through education, training, advocacy and professional collaboration.
